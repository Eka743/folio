# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need a quick one-off document conversion or PDF task, often from a
phone or a browser on a personal computer. They want to select files, make a
small change or conversion, and download the result without creating an
account or sending private documents to a service.

## Product Purpose

Folio is a free browser toolkit for everyday document and PDF operations. It
helps users complete common file tasks in a short, understandable workflow:
select or drop a file, process it locally, validate the result, and download
it. Success means the tool feels trustworthy, fast, and clear about both its
capabilities and its limits.

## Brand Personality

Simple, calm, honest. Folio should feel dependable and lightweight, with
privacy communicated through the product behavior and concise copy rather than
fear-based messaging. Beta or unsupported capabilities should be labelled
plainly.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble upload-first document farms, ad-heavy converter sites, noisy
SaaS dashboards, or interfaces that hide limitations behind generic success
messages. Do not imitate another tool's branding, assets, colors, or exact
layout.

## Design Principles

- Keep files local and make that boundary visible.
- Show the next useful action without adding workflow ceremony.
- Prefer honest capability labels over optimistic placeholders.
- Use familiar controls with strong keyboard and touch affordances.
- Make output and failure states understandable enough to recover quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, status and error
announcements, readable filenames with full-name access, and touch targets
large enough for mobile use. Preserve meaning without relying on color alone.
Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid page-level horizontal overflow, and
keep document previews decorative to assistive technology when the filename
and format already provide the semantic information.
